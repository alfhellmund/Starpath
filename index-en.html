Starpath

Nautical almanac and celestial navigation for iPad, iPhone and Mac

Other languages

Starpath is a digital nautical almanac modelled on the printed Nautisches Jahrbuch (German Nautical Almanac). All astronomical calculations run on the device — Starpath works completely without an internet connection.

Features

  • Daily pages with GHA and declination of the Sun, Moon, planets and stars
  • Celestial navigation: enter sights, compute lines of position and the observed position, show them on a chart
  • DR track and logbook with voyages
  • GPS check: comparing the GPS position with the observed position
  • Polaris Latitude, Star Chart, Planets, Rise & Set, Moon Phases, Phenomena, Corrections, Interpolation
  • PDF export of almanac pages (up to a whole year), correction tables, a blank worksheet for working by hand, and rounds of sights
  • Offline world map included (Natural Earth)

Notice

Starpath is a computational aid for celestial navigation and not an approved navigation device. The calculated values replace neither official nautical charts nor approved navigation equipment. Responsibility for safe navigation remains with the person in command of the vessel.

Privacy

Starpath does not transmit any data. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking. Data leaves the device only when you pass it on yourself — as a diagnostics report, PDF export or support email. More in the privacy policy.
